Give Claude, Codex, and other MCP agents eyes on your electronics. Watch the microscope live while your agent inspects snapshots and marks the component, pin, or test point it means.

## Downloads

- **Windows 11 x64:** `Electroscope-Windows-x64-Setup.exe`, or the portable EXE for use without installation.
- **Mac with Apple Silicon:** `Electroscope-macOS-arm64.dmg`. Open it and drag Electroscope into Applications. A ZIP is also available.

Both apps are signed as UnlikeOtherAI s.r.o.; the Mac app is notarized by Apple. SHA-256 checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

## Start looking together

Plug in your USB microscope, open Electroscope, and select the camera. Click **Copy prompt for agent** and paste it into Claude Code, Codex, or another MCP-capable agent.

You and your agent can draw circles, arrows, crosshairs, boxes, and labels on the same image. Zoom, rotate, freeze, capture close-ups, and save photos as you work through the board.

The MCP server allows access from your trusted network by default and has no password. Disable network access in Settings if your agent runs on the same computer.
